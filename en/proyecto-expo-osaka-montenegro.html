Expo 2025 Osaka Montenegro

Japan

Exhibition and Retail Design

Design of the Montenegro Pavilion for Expo Osaka 2025 as an immersive experience that seamlessly integrates space, content, and interaction. The project combines exhibition design, environmental graphics, and interactive displays, allowing visitors to explore the country's different regions while an immersive ceiling projection enhances the atmosphere throughout the journey. The proposal is completed by a scale model of the Bay of Kotor, conceived as a tool for discovering the territory and understanding its landscape. A project that brings together design, storytelling, and technology to create a contemporary exhibition experience.
