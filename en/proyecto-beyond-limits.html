Beyond Limits

United Arab Emirates

Brand Strategy

Branding development for Beyond Limits, a consultancy specialised in projects, services and solutions within the world of International Expositions and the BIE environment.

The project develops a bold and contemporary visual identity built around the idea of pushing boundaries and constant evolution. The system combines a yellow, black, white and grey colour palette with a flexible graphic language, applied across different brand touchpoints including corporate materials, presentations, merchandising, signage and digital assets. The identity was also adapted to a range of formats and applications, maintaining a consistent and recognisable brand presence.
