Expo 2025 Osaka Mozambique

Japan

Exhibition and Retail Design

Design of the Mozambique Pavilion for Expo 2025 Osaka, under the concept “Expanding Horizons”. The exhibition explores the role of artificial intelligence and robotics as tools to drive the country’s development, connecting education, work, innovation, and sustainability. The visitor journey is structured around different stages that showcase Mozambique’s potential, success stories, current challenges, and future oriented projects, creating an experience that combines storytelling, technology, and knowledge sharing to present a contemporary vision of the country.
