Wonderlust 108

Spain

Visual Identity

Global rebranding for Wanderlust 108, the internationally recognized mindful triathlon.

The new brand identity is inspired by the meaning of 108, a number considered sacred that appears throughout yoga, nature, and astrology. It is represented as an interwoven infinity symbol, expressing connection and unity while reflecting the sense of togetherness at the heart of Wanderlust 108.
