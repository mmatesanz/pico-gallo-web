Expo 2025 Osaka Peru

Japan

Exhibition and Retail Design

Design of the Peru Pavilion for Expo 2025 Osaka, awarded the Gold Prize for Best Exhibition Design by the Bureau International des Expositions. The pavilion's graphic identity draws on the Stepped Meander, an ancient geometric motif present in Peruvian culture.

Its distinctive stepped form becomes the basis of the visual language, creating a contemporary interpretation of Peru's cultural heritage and giving the identity a recognisable, solid and versatile character. Each zone of the pavilion had its own colour and iconography, creating a differentiated visual identity for each space and accompanying visitors throughout the journey. This graphic system connected the different experiences while giving each chapter of the pavilion its own personality.
