Zoo Aquarium Collection

Spain

Visual Identity

Collection design for Zoo Aquarium Madrid, developed across two distinct lines: an animal collection aimed at adults and another focused on children. The project included creating illustrations and patterns inspired by the park’s species, applied to different merchandising products and formats. Audiovisual capsules were also developed for the screens in the stores, including content recording and editing.
