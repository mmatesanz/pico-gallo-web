Rise

Australia

Visual Identity

Brand identity for Rise by Wanderlust, a program designed to inspire people to begin each day with balance and intention.

Created as a traveling wellness experience across Australia, Rise encourages mindful movement, presence, and connection. The visual identity is inspired by the energy of sunrise, using a light and uplifting aesthetic that reflects the program's philosophy of starting each day with clarity, purpose, and renewed energy.
