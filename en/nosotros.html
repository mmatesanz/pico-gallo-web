About

Pico de Gallo is a brand consultancy that turns strategic thinking into brands people recognize, understand, and remember.

We uncover the idea at the heart of every business, define what makes it unique, and bring it to life through its identity, communication, spaces, and experiences.

Because a brand has to do much more than look good. It has to generate meaning, connect with people, and become a powerful force for growth.

We're the ingredient that gives character to every idea.

Something happens when you taste something you already know, but it suddenly tastes different.
You can't quite tell what it is.
You can't point to a single ingredient.
But something changed.

The flavor is deeper.
The experience is more intense.
Memory kicks in.
What seemed familiar becomes special.

That's what we set out to do with design.

At Pico de Gallo we believe great ideas don't always come from inventing everything from scratch. Sometimes they come from finding that invisible ingredient that transforms perception: the detail that sparks emotion, the gesture that gives character, the story that connects, the aesthetic that makes something feel like your own.

That ingredient isn't always visible.
But it's felt.

It's what stops a brand from being just another one.
What stops a space from being just a place.
What stops a presentation from being just information.
What turns an experience into a memory.

Pico de Gallo is that unexpected touch.
That flavor you can't quite explain, but can't forget either.

The secret ingredient that turns an idea into an experience.
