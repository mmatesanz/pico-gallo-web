Expo 2015 Milan

Italy

Visual Identity

The Colombia Pavilion at Expo Milan 2015 featured a dynamic facade designed to transform the pavilion into an ever-changing visual experience. The project involved the creation and production of more than 1,500 final artworks, carefully designed and coordinated across the entire facade of the pavilion.

From every viewpoint, visitors discovered a different composition inspired by Colombia's extraordinary flora and fauna. The result was a dynamic skin that continuously revealed new images as visitors moved around the pavilion, creating an immersive, ever-changing expression of Colombia's natural wealth.
