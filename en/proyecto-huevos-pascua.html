Easter Eggs

Spain

Creative Implementation

Design and production of a collection of Easter eggs for Zoo Aquarium Madrid, including the egg designs, their fabrication, the activity card and promotional flyer. The activity invited children to find different animal eggs hidden throughout the park, each featuring a letter. Once they collected all the letters and formed the secret word, they received a prize.
