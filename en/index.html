Projects

108 Wanderlust

Rebranding

Global rebranding for Wanderlust 108, the world-renowned mindful triathlon.

The new identity is inspired by the meaning of 108, a sacred number deeply rooted in yoga, nature, and astrology. It is represented as an interwoven infinity symbol, expressing unity, connection, and the shared spirit that defines the Wanderlust 108 experience.

Abu Dhabi
International Boat Show

Rebranding

Rebranding of the Abu Dhabi International Boat Show, the leading maritime lifestyle event in the region.

We redesigned its visual identity to reinforce the event's prestige and reflect the excellence of an industry that brings together the world's leading nautical brands.

Parque Warner Madrid

Merchandising

Over the years, we have developed a wide range of projects for the park, including attraction illustrations, graphic design, and merchandising patterns, as well as logos for various retail stores.

We also created complete visual identities for commercial spaces, covering both graphic concepts and interior design. Our collaboration extended to the development of special products, including games and themed editions, expanding the park's retail offering while strengthening its visual universe.

DEVCON VI

Event Design

Graphic and event design for Devcon VI.

The visual identity was built around a graphic language that reflects innovation, collaboration, and technology. Geometric motifs and clean compositions convey order and sophistication, while dynamic elements reinforce the energy and creative spirit of the Ethereum community.
