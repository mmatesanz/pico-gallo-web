108 Wanderlust

Rebranding

Global rebranding for Wanderlust 108, the world-renowned mindful triathlon.
The new branding draws on the meaning of 108, a number considered sacred that appears constantly in yoga, nature, and astrology. It's represented as an interlocking infinity shape, symbolizing union and unity, reflecting the sense of connection that defines Wanderlust 108.

International Boat Show

Rebranding

Rebranding for the Abu Dhabi International Boat Show, the event that defines maritime lifestyle in the region.

Parque Warner Madrid

Merchandising

Over the years we've developed numerous projects for the park, including ride illustrations, graphic design, and patterns for its merchandise lines, as well as creating logos for different shops. We also designed complete visual identities for retail spaces, covering both the graphic concept and the interior design. Our collaboration also included developing special products, such as games and themed editions, which expanded the park's offering and reinforced its visual universe.

DEVCON VI

Event Design

Graphic and event design for Devcon VI.

The visual identity was built around a graphic language that reflects innovation, collaboration, and technology. Geometric motifs and clean compositions convey order and sophistication, while dynamic elements reinforce the energy and creative spirit of the Ethereum community.
