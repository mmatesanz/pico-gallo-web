Expo 2020 Dubai

United Arab Emirates

Exhibition and Retail Design

Design and production of pavilions for several countries at Expo 2020 Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

A project bringing together the work carried out for nine national pavilions: Suriname, Grenada, Saint Kitts and Nevis, San Marino, Malawi, Djibouti, Zimbabwe, Myanmar and Sri Lanka. Several pieces include audiovisual content that could not be incorporated into this static prototype.
