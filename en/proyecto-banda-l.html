Banda-L

Spain

Brand Strategy

Branding design for Banda L, an independent boutique agency and production company focused on developing effective ideas and delivering projects with a close, personalised approach.

The project develops a contemporary, editorial visual identity built around the contrast of black, white and turquoise blue. The system is applied across multiple brand touchpoints, including merchandising, presentations, corporate materials and digital assets, creating a cohesive and recognisable identity across different formats.
